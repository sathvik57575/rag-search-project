# Company Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

## Remote Work Policy
- Core Working Hours: All team members are expected to be available between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM local time for syncs and meetings.
- Remote Eligibility: Full-time employees after completing 90 days of tenure are eligible for up to 3 days per week of remote work.
- Equipment & Expense: The company provides a standard workstation allowance of up to $1,000 every two years.

## Task Escalation Policy
- Overdue Tasks: Any task that remains incomplete more than 3 business days past its `dueDate` must be flagged for escalation to the Project Manager.
- Blocked Tasks: If a task status is changed to `blocked` for more than 48 hours, the assignee must notify the project owner and document the dependency in a project update.
- High Priority Bugs: Severity-1 bugs must be triaged within 4 hours and resolved within 24 hours.

## Communication Guidelines
- Sync Frequency: Project updates must be submitted at least once per week by every project owner.
- Documentation: Major design changes or architectural decisions must be documented in official project documentation prior to implementation.
