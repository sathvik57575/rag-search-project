# Service Level Agreements (SLA) and Compliance

## SLA Response and Resolution Times
- Priority 1 (Critical): Acknowledge within 1 hour; resolution targeted within 1 business day.
- Priority 2 (High): Acknowledge within 4 hours; resolution targeted within 3 business days.
- Priority 3 (Medium): Acknowledge within 24 hours; resolution targeted within 7 business days.
- Priority 4 (Low): Resolution targeted within 14 business days.

## Data Governance and Role Permissions
- Access Control Roles: `employee`, `project_manager`, `admin`.
- Regular Employee: Access restricted to personal profile, owned projects, and assigned tasks.
- Project Manager: Access to all project metrics, updates, workload reports, and employee lists across projects.
- Administrator: Authorized to mutate task statuses, reassign tasks, and modify project update risk levels upon explicit confirmation (`confirmed: true`).
